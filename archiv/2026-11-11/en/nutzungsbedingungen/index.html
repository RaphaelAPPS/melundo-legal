§ 2 Services

Melundo lets you discover, save and plan leisure places and events, suggest places, share photos, videos and reviews, create events, build friendships and groups and exchange messages. Information on places, opening hours, prices and weather comes partly from open data sources (e.g. OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo) or is estimated; it may be incomplete or out of date. Please check important details with the provider before a visit. Melundo does not broker contracts with places or organisers and is not the organiser of events created by users.

An account is required for features involving other people’s content (friends, chats, groups, events, reviews, uploads). Use is permitted from the age of 16. Provide truthful information, in particular your year of birth, keep your password secret and use your account only yourself. There is no entitlement to an account or a particular username; reserved or misleading usernames may be refused.

You are responsible for your content (texts, photos, videos, voice messages, events, reviews, suggested places). The rights to it remain with you. Photos and videos of places are removed when you delete your account.

§ 8 Reporting, moderation and measures

Reporting: via “Report” on places, photos, reviews, messages, chats, events and profiles, via “Report illegal content” (also without an account) or by email to melundoAPP@gmail.com. You can block people in your friends list, in a chat (long-press a message) or when reporting. Review before publication: suggested places, photos and videos of places and public events are reviewed before publication. They may be rejected if they violate § 7 or do not fit (e.g. not a publicly accessible place, duplicate, obviously false information, picture unrelated to the place). Automated tools: publicly visible texts (reviews, events, profile and group names, suggested places) are compared with a list of clearly unacceptable terms when saved; a match only prevents saving. Technical limits also apply, e.g. to the number of messages, uploads, events and requests, to file formats and sizes, and to reserved usernames. Private chat messages are not checked automatically. Decisions on measures are always taken by a person. Measures – taking into account the circumstances of the individual case and the fundamental rights of everyone involved, in particular freedom of expression: not publishing content, removing content, suspending an account temporarily (e.g. 7 or 30 days) or, for serious or repeated violations, indefinitely. Statement of reasons: for every measure you receive a statement of reasons with the measure, the occasion, the rule or law violated, whether it was based on a report, and your options for redress – in the app under “Messages from Melundo”, and for an account suspension also by email (Art. 17 DSA). Objection: you can object to any decision at any time via “Help & contact” or by email. A person reviews the decision again and informs you of the outcome with reasons. Recourse to the ordinary courts remains unaffected. People who report receive a confirmation of receipt and the outcome of their report. We report criminal offences that threaten the life or safety of persons to the Federal Criminal Police Office (Art. 18 DSA).

Whoever creates an event is its organiser and is responsible for accurate information (time, place, price, participant limit) and for complying with applicable rules, e.g. protection of minors (alcohol, tobacco, access for minors), permits, the house rules of the venue, noise protection and hygiene. A price is information only; Melundo does not process payments. Contracts are concluded only between organisers and participants. Private events are visible only to invited people and chat members. Public events are reviewed before publication; this review does not replace checking the legality on site. When meeting people you only know from the app: meet in public places, tell someone and respect your limits. In an emergency: call 112 (police in Germany: 110). Statutory claims against the provider remain unaffected.
