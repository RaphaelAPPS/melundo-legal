Community guidelines

Version: 1 November 2026 (2026-11-01)

Archived version of 2026-11-01. Go to the current version

What this is about

Melundo is meant to connect people and make shared activities possible. So that everyone feels safe, these rules apply – in addition to the terms of use.

Stay respectful

No insults, threats, targeted harassment, bullying or attacks because of origin, skin colour, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age. Criticism in reviews is allowed if it is factual and based on your own experience.

Be honest

No fake identities, no fake reviews, no events with false information, no bait offers. Suggest only places that really exist and are publicly accessible.

Protect privacy

Do not publish other people’s addresses, phone numbers, pictures or other personal information without their permission. Share your live location only with people you trust.

Photos and videos

Only your own or authorised recordings. Identifiable people only with their consent, children and teenagers only with their parents’ consent. No nudity, no sexual content, no violence. Photos of places should show the place. They are reviewed before publication.

Meet safely

No illegal or dangerous activities. Respect other people’s limits and a “no”. Meet people you only know from the app in public places first. In an emergency: call 112 (police in Germany: 110).

No spam and no advertising

No mass messages, chain letters, fraudulent offers or advertising for your own products and services.

Report and block

You can report places, photos, reviews, messages, chats, events and people via “Report”. You can block people in your friends list, in a chat (long-press a message) or when reporting. If you block a person, they can no longer send you friend requests, invite you or add you to groups, can no longer see your profile and can no longer write to you in your previous direct chat. In groups you share, the app hides their messages for you; the other members still see them. You can undo this under Profile → Privacy & safety. You can also report illegal content under “Report illegal content” or to melundoAPP@gmail.com.

What happens in case of violations

Violations may lead to content not being published or being removed, or to an account being suspended temporarily or indefinitely. You always receive reasons and can object (§ 8 of the terms of use).
