Report illegal content

You can notify Melundo of content you consider illegal (Art. 16 DSA). Please state: • the content concerned as precisely as possible (e.g. name of the place, event, group or profile, date of a message), • why you consider the content illegal (ideally citing the law), • your name and email address for replies, • confirmation that your information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge. In the app you can also do this directly via “Report” on the content.

Without contact details

Reports of offences against children (e.g. sexual abuse, depictions of abuse) can also be sent anonymously without name and email address.

What happens next

We confirm receipt immediately in the app and, if you gave an email address, also by email. A person reviews every report carefully, objectively and promptly – there is no automated assessment. We inform you of the decision by email or (for reports via “Report” in the app) under “Messages from Melundo”, together with your options for redress. No reply is possible for anonymous reports. A report does not automatically lead to removal. We pass on information about a criminal offence that threatens the life or safety of persons to the German Federal Criminal Police Office (Art. 18 DSA, Section 13 DDG). In an emergency: call 112 (police in Germany: 110).

Without the app
