Delete account

Version: 11 November 2026 (2026-11-11)

How to delete your account in the app

1. Open your profile. 2. Choose “Settings”. 3. Open “Account & privacy”. 4. Choose “Delete account”. 5. Confirm your decision. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Tip: before that, you can save a copy of your data under “Privacy requests” → “Download my data”.

Without the app

Write to melundoAPP@gmail.com from your account’s email address with the subject “Delete account”. We delete the account without undue delay, at the latest within one month, and confirm the deletion.

What is deleted

Profile, settings, saved places, calendar, visits, reviews, photos and videos, photo memories, profile picture, friendships and blocks, chat memberships, your messages including photos, videos and voice messages, reactions and votes, your events including media, acceptances and invitations, your reports and notices, notifications, read positions, busy days, Surprise Me answers, your Moment Capsules and capsule photos, suggested places not yet approved and your access to the sign-in service.

What remains

Suggested places that have already been approved remain as place information without any link to you. Tasks and cost entries paid by others remain for the group without your name. Open requests to us remain until they are handled. Reports by others about you remain stored (period: privacy policy section 19). A log entry “account deleted” without personal reference is removed after 90 days. Data may remain in our hosting provider’s daily backups for up to 7 days.

Without the app

Send us an email – also without an account. The app additionally offers a form.
