Third-party services used

Version: 1 November 2026 (2026-11-01)

Supabase

Purpose: database, sign-in, file storage, real-time messages, server functions, sign-in emails (processor, Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore; sub-processors including in the USA). Data: all account data and content, IP address. Storage location: Ireland (EU). Transfer: EU standard contractual clauses.

Wikimedia Commons

Source of freely licensed photos of places. The photos are stored as copies in our storage (Supabase, Ireland) – no data is transmitted to Wikimedia when they are displayed.

Open-Meteo

Purpose: weather (OpenMeteo GmbH, Switzerland). Data: location rounded to approx. 1 km, IP address.

Nominatim / OpenStreetMap

Purpose: address search for events (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom). Data: address text entered, IP address.

Google (Gmail)

Purpose: sending sign-in emails via melundoAPP@gmail.com (Google Ireland Limited). Data: email address, email content. Otherwise no Google web services; links to Google Maps or Google Calendar only open the respective app or website at your request. There is no data processing agreement for the private Gmail account; a dedicated email service is planned.

OpenStreetMap data

Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), stored in our database – no transfer when displayed.

GeoNames

List of cities and municipalities (CC BY 4.0), included in the app – no transfer.

RevenueCat

Included in the app but deactivated while Melundo is free – no data transfer.

Apple Push Notification service

Purpose: notifications about new chat messages on iPhones (Apple Inc., USA). Data: device token, content of the notification (sender name, group name if applicable; message text only if you turn it on). Only with your permission, can be turned off in the settings. Transfer to the USA based on the EU standard contractual clauses according to Apple.

Not used

Firebase, other push services, advertising, analytics and tracking services, crash reporting, map tiles from an external provider.
