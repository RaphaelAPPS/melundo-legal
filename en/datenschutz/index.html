2. Summary

• No advertising, no tracking, no analytics or crash reporting, no sale of data. • Discovering and searching works without an account. Friends, chats, groups, events, reviews and uploads require an account. • Your exact location is shared only if you explicitly start live location in a chat. • The app removes location data (GPS) from photos and videos before uploading them. • You can download your data in the app at any time and delete your account immediately. • Our data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase. Details on all services are in section 16.

3. Legal bases

This policy is based on the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG) and the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). We process data • to provide the features you use (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), • to comply with legal obligations, for example under the Digital Services Act (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), • based on our legitimate interest in a secure, stable app free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We only need consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for optional features that you switch on yourself in the settings (see sections 6 and 15).

4. Using Melundo without an account

Discover, search, map, weather and all legal texts work without an account. Settings (e.g. selected city, theme, language, interests) are stored on your device only. To show places and public events, the app retrieves them from our hosting provider (section 16); technically your IP address is transmitted. You can also write to us without an account via “Help & contact” and “Report illegal content”.

5. Registration and sign-in

When you register we process: email address, password, display name, optional username and your year of birth. The server also stores an account ID, the time of registration, email confirmation and last sign-in, and which version of the terms of use you accepted and which version of this privacy policy you acknowledged (with time). Purpose: creating and managing your account, sign-in, proof of the accepted terms. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. Year of birth: Melundo is for people aged 16 and over (section 22). The year of birth is used to check your age and to show adults-only places only to adults. It is not visible to anyone and cannot be changed later. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR (protection of minors). Passwords are stored by our hosting provider’s sign-in service (Supabase Auth) only as a hash, never in plain text. Emails: confirming your email address and resetting your password work via emails that Supabase’s sign-in service sends through our email account melundoAPP@gmail.com (Gmail, Google). Content: your email address and a confirmation link (section 16). We do not send any other emails (e.g. newsletters). On your device, the app stores the sign-in session in the app’s own storage so that you stay signed in. It is removed when you sign out.

6. Profile, friends and visibility

Your profile (display name, username, profile picture) is visible to people you are friends with or share a chat with. If you switch on “Discoverable” (off by default), all signed-in users can find you via search and see your profile. People you block or who block you cannot see your profile. Interests from the introduction stay on your device. Only if you switch on “Interests visible to friends” (off by default) are they saved in your profile and shown to friends as “shared interests”; switching it off removes them (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, can be withdrawn at any time). Reviews are public and show your display name. We store friendships, invitations and blocks so that these features work. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Places, saved places, calendar and reviews

We process saved places, calendar entries with reminder times, visits (“I was here”), challenges, reviews, places you suggest, photos and videos of places and photo memories. Suggested places and photos and videos of places are reviewed by us before publication and are then public; photo memories are private. Reminders for dates are scheduled locally on your device. For this the app asks for permission to send notifications; on Android it may restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts. No push service is used for these reminders. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8. Events

We process events you create (title, description, date, place or address, pictures and videos, participant limit, price information), acceptances and declines, and invitations. Public events are reviewed before publication and are then visible to everyone in the app – also without an account. Private events are visible only to you, invited people and the members of a chat in which you share them; this is enforced by the database. Anyone who can see an event can also see which accounts have accepted; names and profile pictures appear only within the profile visibility described in section 6. If you enter an address and tap “Search”, the text you entered is sent to the address service Nominatim (section 16). Please do not enter a private home address for public events. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

9. Chats and groups

We process message content, senders, times, chat and group memberships and roles, shared photos, videos and voice messages, polls, dates, ideas, tasks, cost splitting, meeting points and reactions. Messages can only be read by the members of the respective chat; this is enforced by the database (row level security). Direct chats are only possible between friends; when someone accepts a friend request, the server automatically creates your shared direct chat (without messages yet). Transmission is encrypted with TLS. There is no end-to-end encryption – technically the operator can access stored messages. In the app, the operator sees the text of a message only if it has been reported (section 13) or if there is a legal obligation. Private messages are not checked automatically. To prevent spam, messages are limited to 30 per minute per person. Deleting and editing: “Delete for me” only stores for your account which message is hidden. “Delete for everyone” removes the text, file and references; a “deleted” note with its time remains. Edited messages are marked “edited” with the time; the previous version is not stored. Forwarded messages are stored as a copy in the target chat and marked. Pinned messages are visible to all members. Delivered and read: for each chat we store until when messages have arrived on your device and until when you have read them, plus a read history (at most 100 points in time per chat) so the sender can see when their message was read. If you turn off “Read receipts” (Profile → Privacy & safety), only a private read position for the unread counter is stored, the read history is deleted – and you no longer see other people’s read receipts either. In-app notifications: for friend requests, accepted requests, when someone adds you to a group and for event invitations, the server stores a notification (type, triggering person, reference, times; at most 200 per account) and – if you allow notifications and have not turned that type off – sends a notification via Apple. You can turn off each type individually. Surprise Me: if you enter mood, interests, budget, distance and time in a group, only your account can see these answers; the group only sees how many have answered and combined suggestions. We also store the starting point of the round (city or the starting person’s location rounded to about 100 m). The answers are deleted when the round is finished. Moment Capsules: memories (title, date, optionally place/event and group) with photos and videos. New files are only visible to the person who uploaded them; only when you explicitly share a photo do members of a group capsule see it. Profile: optionally a short description. Group members see your profile even if you are not friends. In other people’s profiles you see shared groups as well as places and media shared in your common chats. Notifications for new messages (iPhone): if you allow notifications, the app registers your device with the server. We store the Apple Push Notification service device token, language, the “Show message text” setting and timestamps. When someone sends a message to one of your chats, our server sends a notification via Apple to your device (section 16) – not for muted chats and not from people you have blocked or who have blocked you. Content: the sender’s name, for groups the group name and – as long as “Show message text” is on (default: on) – the message text (shortened), otherwise only the type of message (e.g. “Photo”). On a locked device iOS hides name and text and only shows “New message” if Settings → Notifications → “Show Previews” is set to “When Unlocked”. So that each message is notified only once, the server notes for which message a notification has been sent. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app settings or on your iPhone. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for spam protection Art. 6(1)(f); access to the device token under Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

10. Location

You choose a city, “All of Germany” or “My location”. Only with “My location” does the app ask for location permission and determine the approximate location of your device. The exact position stays on the device. For radius search, search and nearby events the app sends a point rounded to about 100 metres to our database; it is not stored there. For the weather, a point rounded to about 1 kilometre is sent to Open-Meteo (section 16). Live location in a chat: only if you explicitly start it is your exact position sent to the members of that chat about every 45 seconds for the chosen duration (at most 8 hours) – only while the app is open. You can stop it at any time. After it ends, the position is deleted at the latest during the next hourly clean-up. There is no location tracking in the background. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (feature requested by you); access to your device’s location data is based on Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG because it is strictly necessary for the feature you requested.

11. Permissions on your device

The app asks for a permission only when you use the related feature. Without the permission, the rest of the app keeps working. You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time. • Location: for “My location” and live location (section 10). • Camera: to take photos or videos directly in the app – only while recording. • Microphone: for voice messages and video sound – only while recording. • Photos/gallery: to choose pictures or videos you want to upload. The app reads only the files you select. • Calendar (iPhone): only to add an event that you add yourself – via the iOS calendar dialog, in which you confirm the entry. Melundo does not read your calendar or your contacts. • Notifications: for locally scheduled reminders (section 7) – including one hour before group meetups and events you joined –, notifications about new chat messages and in-app notifications (section 9). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG.

12. Photos, videos and voice messages

Uploaded files are stored in our hosting provider’s file storage. Only certain image, video and audio formats with a limited file size are allowed, and there are daily limits. Location data: many phones store the place where a photo or video was taken in the file. The app makes this GPS data unreadable before uploading. Other metadata (e.g. time of recording, device type) may remain in the file. Visibility: photos and videos of places and public events are public after review; media in chats are visible only to the chat members; profile pictures to the people described in section 6; photo memories only to you. Please upload only content you hold the rights to, and show other people only with their consent (terms of use § 6). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

13. Reports, moderation and suspensions

Review before publication: suggested places, photos and videos of places and public events are reviewed by a person before they appear. Word filter: when publicly visible texts are saved (reviews, events, profile and group names, suggested places), the server automatically compares them with a list of clearly unacceptable terms. A match only prevents saving; nothing is stored or analysed. Reports: when you report something, we store your account ID, the reported content (as an identifier), the reason and the time. The reported person does not learn who reported them. To review it, the operator sees the reported content, for messages the text of the reported message. Decisions: if your content is not published or is removed, or your account is suspended, we store the decision with its reasons and show it to you under “Messages from Melundo”; we also inform you about a suspension by email. For a suspension, a suspension date is set in the sign-in service and active sessions are ended. If you report something, we inform you of the outcome. We must report information about criminal offences that threaten the life or safety of persons to the German Federal Criminal Police Office (Art. 18 DSA, Section 13 DDG). There is no automated individual decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 16, 17 and 18 DSA, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a safe platform). Retention: section 19.

14. Contact form, privacy requests and reports of illegal content

If you write to us via a form or by email, we process your details (e.g. email address, name, subject, message, the content concerned) and, if you are signed in, your account ID in order to handle and answer your request. In certain cases, reports of illegal content can also be sent anonymously. To prevent spam, requests are limited to 5 per hour; for this we store only a value that changes daily instead of the IP address, and delete it after 7 days. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b), (c) (e.g. Art. 12 and 15 GDPR, Art. 16 DSA) and (f) GDPR.

15. Storage on your device, no tracking

The app stores on your device only what is strictly necessary for the features you use (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG): settings, sign-in session, cache for places and pictures (pictures at most 21 days), locally scheduled reminders. No consent is required for this. Suggestions based on places you viewed (counted on the device only) are off by default; you can switch them on in the settings and off again at any time – the counts are then deleted. Melundo 4You: only when these suggestions are on, the app also learns from your search terms and the places you view, save and plan, and remembers “Not interested” and how often a suggestion was shown. All of this stays on your device, is not transmitted and is deleted when you switch it off or tap “Reset”. Rewards (points, level, badges) are not stored separately on the server: they are calculated when needed from your approved photos, visits, reviews and tips. Friends and group members only see them while “Show badges on my profile” is on. Busy days in the calendar are stored on the server without any content; friends only learn on which days everyone is free – and only if you turn on “Share free days” (default: off). No tracking, advertising or analytics technologies and no advertising IDs are used.

19. Retention

Principle: we store account data and content as long as your account exists. If you delete content or your account, it is removed from the database immediately (exceptions when deleting the account: section 20). Fixed periods (automatic hourly clean-up): • live location: at most 8 hours, then at the latest 1–2 hours until deletion • spam check values for requests: 7 days • Melundo security logs (e.g. failed account deletion, without content): 90 days • completed requests (contact, privacy, reports of illegal content): 12 months after completion • completed in-app reports: 12 months after receipt • messages from Melundo (statements of reasons): 12 months • expired account suspensions: 12 months after expiry At our hosting provider (Supabase, Pro plan): access logs (section 21) 7 days; daily database backups 7 days. Deleted data may therefore remain in backups for up to 7 days; backups are used only to restore data after a fault. Device tokens for notifications: until you sign out, turn notifications off, Apple reports the token as invalid or you delete your account. The note “notification sent” is deleted together with the message. On your device: picture cache at most 21 days; all local data is deleted with the app.

20. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account & privacy → Delete account) or by email. Deletion takes effect immediately. Deleted: profile, settings, saved places, calendar, visits, reviews, photos and videos of places, photo memories, profile picture, friendships, blocks, chat memberships, your messages with photos, videos and voice messages, reactions and votes, your events with media, acceptances, invitations, your ideas in groups, cost entries you paid, suggested places not yet approved, your reports, notices from Melundo, suspension records, accepted versions of the legal texts, completed requests and your access to the sign-in service. Remaining without any link to your account: suggested places that have already been approved (as place information); tasks and cost entries in groups that you created but others paid (without your name); in cost splits paid by others, an identifier no longer linked to any account so that the group’s accounts stay correct; open requests to us until they are handled (with the email address given there); reports by others about you until the period in section 19 expires; a log entry “account deleted” without personal reference (90 days). Backups at the hosting provider: up to 7 days (section 19).

21. Technical data and security

Each connection to our hosting provider technically involves the IP address, time, requested address and request header details (e.g. the software library used), which the hosting provider logs (7 days, section 19). Melundo itself stores security-relevant events without content (90 days). Safeguards: encrypted transmission (TLS), access rules directly in the database (row level security), passwords only as a hash, private files only via time-limited links, rate limits against abuse, removal of GPS data from media. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security, stability, prevention of abuse).

22. Minimum age and minors

Melundo is for people aged 16 and over. When registering, you confirm your age and enter your year of birth; the server rejects accounts with a year of birth indicating an age under 16. In Germany, young people aged 16 and over can consent to the processing of their data by online services themselves (Art. 8 GDPR). Adults-only places are shown only to signed-in adults. If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 16, we delete it. Parents or guardians can write to us at melundoAPP@gmail.com.

23. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. In the app: “Download my data” (a copy of your stored data as a file), edit profile, delete account, privacy request form. Or by email to melundoAPP@gmail.com. We reply within one month. Email address, password and year of birth are required for an account; without them you can only use the features available without an account. All other details are optional.

24. Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the authority responsible for us: Thüringer Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Häßlerstraße 8, 99096 Erfurt, Germany – https://www.tlfdi.de Your right to complain to other supervisory authorities, e.g. where you live, remains unaffected.
