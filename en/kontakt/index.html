Help, contact & privacy requests

Version: 1 November 2026 (2026-11-01)

Help & contact

Questions, feedback or a bug? Write to us using the form – also without an account – or by email to melundoAPP@gmail.com. For bugs, a short description with your device and app version (see settings) helps. Please never send passwords.

Objecting to a decision

You disagree with a decision by Melundo (e.g. removed content or a suspension)? Choose the matching topic in the form or reply to the notice. A person reviews the decision again.

Your data protection rights

Would you like information about your stored data or to exercise a data protection right (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent)? Write to melundoAPP@gmail.com or use the form below.

Right away in the app

When signed in, you can get a copy of your data stored with Melundo as a file under “Download my data”. You change profile details in your profile and delete your account under “Account & privacy”.

Handling

We answer requests without undue delay, at the latest within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR). So that no data reaches unauthorised persons, we answer requests about an account only to that account’s email address or in the app.

Without the app

Send us an email – also without an account. The app additionally offers a form.
